What the islanders say

Honest islanders only say true things. Liars only say false things. Tap a portrait to note your suspicion.

    How to play

    The treasure is buried on one tile. It is never under a landmark.

    Each islander is either honest or a liar. Honest islanders tell only the truth; liars say only false things, every time. You are told how many of them lie.

    Work out who is lying, then use the true statements to find the tile. Cross off tiles as you rule them out, switch to Dig here, and tap the tile. You have three shovels.

    Reading the clues: "north of the Hut" means any row above the Hut's row. "Right next to" includes diagonals. "Within 2 steps" counts diagonal steps as one. The coast is the outer ring of tiles. The "northern half" is the top half of the rows.

    Every island has exactly one answer, so you never need to guess.
